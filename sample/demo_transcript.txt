Sample run — demo fallback
===========================
Use this if live wifi / mic misbehaves during the interview. Read the agent lines
aloud (or play a screen recording) and show the matching row appear in the Sheet by
running:  python -m agent.sheets   (appends a fake "misheard-order" row)

--- LOW-RATING CALL (feedback collected) ---
Agent:    Hi! Thanks for stopping by. Please say the store number.
Customer: Three oh three.
Agent:    Store 303, Fazoli's in Lexington — is that right?
Customer: Yep.
Agent:    Great. How would you rate your experience from 1 to 10?
Customer: Like a four.
Agent:    Sorry to hear that — what could we have done better?
Customer: The AI kept mishearing me and added breadsticks I didn't order, and I had
          to repeat my drink three times.
Agent:    Got it, thank you for the honest feedback. Have a great day!

  -> Row logged:
     store_id=303  brand=Fazoli's  region=Southeast  rating=4
     summary="Order was misheard; unwanted breadsticks added and drink repeated."
     issue_tag=misheard-order   sentiment=negative

--- HAPPY CALL (no feedback prompt) ---
Agent:    Hi! Please say the store number.
Customer: One oh one.
Agent:    Store 101, Carl's Jr. in Anaheim — correct?
Customer: Correct.
Agent:    How would you rate your experience from 1 to 10?
Customer: Nine.
Agent:    Awesome, thanks so much! Take care.

  -> Row logged:

     (feedback / summary / issue_tag / sentiment left blank by design)
